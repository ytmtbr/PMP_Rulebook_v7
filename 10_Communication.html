Rule 1　Communication Management Flow

Identify Stakeholders ↓ Analyze Stakeholders ↓ Communications Management Plan ↓ Manage Communications ↓ Monitor Communications
Communication Planから作る。
Stakeholders First.

Rule 2　Communication Plan

Who ↓ What ↓ When ↓ How ↓ Frequency
期待値を書く。 Quality Requirementを書く。
Communication Plan = HOW

Rule 3　Communication Problem

Understand Cause ↓ Clarify Message ↓ Select Better Method ↓ Confirm Understanding
SponsorへEscalate。 Meetingを増やすだけ。
Understand before Escalate.

Rule 4　Virtual Team

Visual Tools ↓ Task Board ↓ Shared Documents ↓ Collaboration
口頭説明を増やす。
Visual beats Verbal.

Rule 5　Language Barrier

Information Radiator ↓ Electronic Task Board ↓ Visual Status
翻訳サービスを最初に導入。
Visual Communication.

Rule 6　Stakeholder Expectations

Listen ↓ Understand ↓ Clarify ↓ Confirm
PMが勝手に判断。
Clarify Expectations.

Rule 7　Communication Method

状況 方法
複雑な議論 Meeting
正式通知 Email
進捗共有 Dashboard
Agile Team Task Board
全部Meeting。
Fit Method to Purpose.

Rule 8　Feedback

Send ↓ Receive ↓ Confirm ↓ Adjust
送ったら終わり。
Communication requires Understanding.

Rule 9　Active Listening

Listen ↓ Clarify ↓ Confirm ↓ Respond
すぐ解決策を言う。
Listen First.

Rule 10　Escalation

Communicate ↓ Clarify ↓ Resolve ↓ Escalate
Escalation First.
Escalation Last.

Rule 11　Reporting

Accurate ↓ Transparent ↓ Objective ↓ Timely
Greenに見せる。 問題を隠す。
Tell the Truth.

Rule 12　Communication Summary

Stakeholders ↓ Communication Plan ↓ Manage ↓ Feedback ↓ Monitor

あなたのFull Examで多かったミス

  • Communication Planを先に選ぶ
  • Meetingだけで解決しようとする
  • Escalationが早い
  • Visual Toolを選ばない
  • Feedback確認を忘れる
Communication問題は Understand Clarify Feedback この3つを最優先。
