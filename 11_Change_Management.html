Rule 1　Change Management Flow

Identify Change ↓ Impact Analysis ↓ Change Request ↓ Review ↓ Approval ↓ Update Baselines ↓ Implement ↓ Verify
PMだけで変更。 先に実装する。
Analyze Before Approve.

Rule 2　まずImpact Analysis

Any Change ↓ Analyze Scope Schedule Cost Quality Risk Resources Stakeholders
すぐMeeting。 すぐChange Request。
Impact Analysis comes first.

Rule 3　Approved Change

Approved ↓ Update PM Plan ↓ Update Baselines ↓ Execute ↓ Verify Effectiveness
承認されたから終了。
Approval ≠ Implementation

Rule 4　Corrective Action

Known Problem ↓ Corrective Action ↓ Verify ↓ Continue
Meetingだけ。 延期する。
Known Problem ↓ Correct

Rule 5　Preventive Action

Potential Future Problem ↓ Preventive Action ↓ Reduce Probability
既に起きた問題にもPreventiveを選ぶ。
Future ↓ Prevent

Rule 6　Defect Repair

Defect Found ↓ Repair ↓ Retest ↓ Accept
Warrantyで直す。 Release優先。
Fix Before Release.

Rule 7　Rebaseline

Major Approved Change ↓ Stakeholder Alignment ↓ Governance Approval ↓ Update Baselines
PMだけでBaseline変更。
Rebaseline requires approval.

Rule 8　Organizational Change

Business Change ↓ Assess Impact ↓ Communicate ↓ Adjust Project
Risk Registerだけ更新。
Business Change ↓ Project Change.

Rule 9　ADKAR

Letter Meaning
A Awareness
D Desire
K Knowledge
A Ability
R Reinforcement
Impact Analysisの代わりに使う。
ADKAR = People Change

Rule 10　Change Impact Assessment

Organizational Change ↓ CIA ↓ Impact Matrix ↓ Response
ADKARを最初に選ぶ。
CIA = Impact First.

Rule 11　Integrated Change Control

Evaluate ↓ Approve / Reject ↓ Update Documents ↓ Communicate ↓ Implement
変更だけ実装。 文書更新しない。
Every Approved Change updates the project.

Rule 12　Change Management Summary

Identify ↓ Impact Analysis ↓ Formal Change ↓ Approval ↓ Update ↓ Implement ↓ Verify

あなたのFull Examで多かったミス

  • Impact Analysisを飛ばす
  • Meetingを選ぶ
  • Risk Registerだけ更新する
  • 承認後の実装確認を忘れる
  • Baseline更新を忘れる
Change問題は Impact Analysis Formal Approval Baseline Update この3つでほぼ解ける。
