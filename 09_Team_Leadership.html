Rule 1　Leadership Flow

Assess Situation ↓ Coach ↓ Facilitate ↓ Empower ↓ Escalate (Last)
Sponsorへ相談。 PMが全部解決。
Coach First.

Rule 2　Servant Leadership

Listen ↓ Coach ↓ Remove Impediments ↓ Support Team ↓ Continuous Improvement
Command & Control Manager になる。
Support, don't command.

Rule 3　Conflict Resolution

Private Discussion ↓ Coaching ↓ Constructive Dialogue ↓ Facilitation ↓ Escalation
公開で注意。 すぐManagerへ。
Private First.

Rule 4　Team Member Conflict

One person asks for advice ↓ Coach ---------------- Whole team conflict ↓ Facilitate Discussion
相手側へ直接注意。
Individual ↓ Coach Team ↓ Facilitate

Rule 5　Working Agreement

Ground Rules ↓ Reminder ↓ Discussion ↓ Agreement ↓ Follow-up
Legal HR Sponsor へ飛ぶ。
Ground Rules First.

Rule 6　Agile Team Behavior

Self-organizing ↓ Transparent ↓ Collaborative ↓ Continuous Improvement
PMが毎日指示。
Self-management.

Rule 7　Coaching

Understand ↓ Question ↓ Guide ↓ Support
答えを教える。 命令する。
Guide, don't solve.

Rule 8　Performance Problems

Understand Cause ↓ Coach ↓ Support ↓ Improve ↓ Escalate if Needed
即評価を下げる。 即交代。
Root Cause First.

Rule 9　Knowledge Transfer

Outgoing Member ↓ Knowledge Transfer ↓ Documentation ↓ Onboarding ↓ Validation
新メンバーだけで読む。
Capture Tacit Knowledge.

Rule 10　Team Development

Stage 特徴
Forming 開始
Storming 衝突
Norming 安定
Performing 高成果
Adjourning 終了
順番を覚えていない。
F S N P A

Rule 11　Motivation

Recognition ↓ Autonomy ↓ Purpose ↓ Growth
お金だけ。
Intrinsic Motivation.

Rule 12　Leadership Summary

Coach ↓ Facilitate ↓ Empower ↓ Escalate Last

あなたのFull Examで多かったミス

  • CoachではなくMeetingを選ぶ
  • Escalationが早い
  • Managerへ相談する
  • PMが直接解決する
  • Ground Rulesを忘れる
Leadership問題は Coach Facilitate Empower この3つを最優先。
